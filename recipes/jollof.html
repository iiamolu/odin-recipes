Jollof Rice

Home

Description

Jollof rice is a nigerian rice dish with a smoky, savory, and tomatoey flavor. It's a staple in West Africa!

Ingredients

  • Parboiled rice
  • Roma Tomatoes
  • Bell Peppers
  • Scotch Bonnet Peppers
  • Onion
  • Tomato Paste
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Chicken Stock

Steps:

  1. Blend the peppers and tomatoes
  2. Heat the oil
  3. Fry the tomato paste
  4. Cook the pepper mixture
  5. Add seasonings
  6. Add chicken stock
  7. Add the rice
  8. Cook the rice
  9. Fluff and Serve
