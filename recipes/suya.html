Suya

Description

Suya is a smoky, savory grilled beef dish originating from Nigeria. Known for its dry pepper covering after it's grilled over fire.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Suya Spice
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Ginger Powder
  • Cayenne Pepper
  • Salt
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Skewers (optional)

Steps:

  1. Slice the beef into thin, bite-sized strips
  2. Prepare the skewers
  3. Mix the seasonings
  4. Season the beef
  5. Skewer the beef
  6. Let the beef marinate
  7. Preheat the grill
  8. Grill suya
  9. Add extra spice
