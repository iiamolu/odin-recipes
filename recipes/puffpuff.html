Home

Description

Puff Puff is like a Nigerian version of a doughnut. It's sweet and doughy. I personally think it's sometimes both sweet and savory!

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Sugar
  • Dry Yeast
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Vegetable Oil

Steps:

  1. Mix the dry ingredients
  2. Add warm water
  3. Let the dough rise
  4. Heat the oil
  5. Shape the puff puff
  6. Fry until golden brown
  7. Remove excess oil
